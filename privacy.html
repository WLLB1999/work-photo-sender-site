Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Work Photo Sender is an Android and Windows application for authorized users to request, capture and email photos and PDF documents. This policy explains what data the application accesses, uses, stores and shares.

Data collected or processed

  • Connected Google accounts: the verified account email address and OAuth credentials required to send messages on the account holder's behalf.
  • Photo requests: recipient address, requested destination inbox, request creation/expiration times, status and, when submitted, a person's name.
  • Emails, photos and documents: the messages, attachments and email addresses selected by the authorized user or a person submitting a requested photo.
  • Operational data: rate-limit counters, authentication metadata and logs used for functionality and security.

Google API access and limited use

When you connect Google, we request openid email to display your verified address and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send to send photo-request invitation emails at your direction. We do not request access to read, search, modify or delete Gmail messages. Google account information is used only to provide or improve this user-facing feature, protect the service, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Human access to Google user data is limited to cases permitted by Google's policies, such as with your affirmative consent, security, legal compliance, or support when necessary.

Where data is stored and shared

Google handles Google-account authorization and Gmail message transmission. Supabase processes account connections, encrypted Google refresh tokens, request metadata and security records. Resend is used to deliver submitted photo attachments and certain other emails or PDF attachments to addresses selected by the authorized user. Recipient mail providers also process delivered messages and attachments and maintain their own retention policies. Service providers are used only as necessary to perform these functions.

Security and control

Google refresh tokens are encrypted before storage on the server and are not exposed to the mobile or desktop app. Authorized owners choose which connected Google sender is used. You may disconnect a Google account in the app; the server removes its stored connection and attempts to revoke Google's refresh token. You can independently revoke access from Google's third-party access settings. No method of transmission or storage is entirely free from risk.

Retention

Photo-request links expire after seven days and can be used only once. Request database records are scheduled to be deleted approximately 30 days after creation. Some local photo-history features retain device-local history for up to seven days. Google sender connections persist until you disconnect or the service is retired. Copies of messages and attachments may remain in sent, received or provider-managed mailboxes subject to their own policies; deleting a request does not delete already-delivered email.

Choices, children's data and contact

Use the app only with accounts and content you are permitted to handle. This app is not directed to children. For questions about this policy or data requests, use the application support address shown in the Google OAuth consent screen. The app operator should keep that address monitored and update this policy if practices change.

Política de Privacidad

Vigente desde el 2 de noviembre de 2026

Work Photo Sender permite solicitar fotos y enviar imágenes o PDF mediante Android y Windows. Procesamos la cuenta de Google verificada, credenciales OAuth necesarias para enviar, direcciones de correo, datos de solicitudes, mensajes y archivos que los usuarios decidan transmitir.

Uso de Google

Usamos openid email para identificar la cuenta y gmail.send únicamente para enviar invitaciones cuando el usuario lo solicite. No solicitamos acceso para leer, buscar, modificar ni eliminar correos de Gmail. No vendemos datos de Google, no los usamos para anuncios personalizados ni para entrenar modelos generales de inteligencia artificial.

Almacenamiento y proveedores

Google procesa la autorización y el envío por Gmail; Supabase almacena credenciales cifradas y registros de solicitudes; Resend procesa otros correos y archivos enviados. Los destinatarios y proveedores de correo pueden conservar mensajes según sus políticas.

Seguridad y conservación

Los tokens de Google se guardan cifrados en el servidor y no se comparten con la app móvil o de escritorio. Los enlaces de carga son de un solo uso, vencen después de siete días y los registros se eliminan aproximadamente 30 días después de su creación. Puedes desconectar Google desde la app o revocar el permiso en Google. El correo ya entregado puede permanecer en los buzones de los participantes.

Contacto

Para consultas utiliza el correo de asistencia de Work Photo Sender mostrado en la pantalla de consentimiento de Google.
