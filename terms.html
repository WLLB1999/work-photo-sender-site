Work Photo Sender is a controlled-use application for requesting, capturing and delivering work photos and documents by email.

Authorized use

You must have permission to access each connected email account and handle any document, image, personal data or recipient address. Follow employer policies and applicable laws. Do not use the app for unauthorized or deceptive email, spam, harassment, malicious files or unlawful purposes.

Connected Google accounts

You choose when to connect or disconnect Google accounts and which sender account to use for an invitation. Google Workspace administrators may block third-party access. Connecting an account does not transfer ownership or control of the underlying mailbox.

Delivery, links and availability

Photo requests use single-use links that expire after seven days. Sending requires an internet connection and third-party services. Delivery is not guaranteed because mail providers may reject or filter messages. Check recipient and delivery addresses before sending.

Privacy and changes

For how account details, documents and requests are handled, read the Privacy Policy. These terms and the application can change to improve security or functionality. For inquiries, use the app support contact shown on Google's OAuth consent screen.
