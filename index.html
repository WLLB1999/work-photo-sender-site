Transparency

Exactly why we request Google access

With your permission, Work Photo Sender uses openid email to identify the Google account you've connected and gmail.send to send photo-request invitations only after you tell the app to send one.

We do not request permission to read, search, edit, or delete messages in your Gmail inbox. Refresh tokens are encrypted on the server and are not given to the Android or Windows app. You can disconnect a linked account.

Photos submitted through upload links and other document emails are delivered through our email service provider. See the Privacy Policy for details on data use, storage, service providers and retention.
